About L. Merrick

L. Merrick writes public-record nonfiction from wire services, court reports, official returns, statutes, and corporate disclosures — plus English–Spanish specialty dictionaries for physicians and a short line of practical AI books.

The method is the same across titles: reconstruct what institutions wrote down; label allegations as allegations; keep unfinished cases unfinished; cite sources that were actually consulted. The books do not invent quotations, do not diagnose private medical conditions, and do not speak for any party, campaign, court, or federation.

What these books are not

They are not authorized biographies. They are not legal advice or medical advice. They are not official FIFA, White House, or prosecutorial products. They are not true-crime recreations. Covers and pages do not use stolen stock photography or manufactured testimonials.
