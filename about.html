About

L. Merrick writes public-record nonfiction from wire services, court reports, official returns, statutes, and corporate disclosures. The Groundwood catalog is a set of independent digital editions — court-record books, political and legal chronicles, one sports-history volume, and short current-affairs briefs.

The method is the same across titles: reconstruct what institutions wrote down; label allegations as allegations; keep unfinished cases unfinished; cite sources that were actually consulted. The books do not invent quotations, do not diagnose private medical conditions, and do not speak for any party, campaign, court, or federation.

What these books are not

They are not authorized biographies. They are not legal advice. They are not official FIFA, White House, or prosecutorial products. They are not true-crime recreations and they do not use stolen stock photography or manufactured testimonials.

Imprint
