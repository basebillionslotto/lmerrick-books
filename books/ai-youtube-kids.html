Make Money with AI: YouTube for Kids (COPPA-Safe)

Series: Make Money with AI

Ebook · $3.99 USD · L. Merrick

You can teach children on YouTube without turning childhood into a loophole.

Make Money with AI: YouTube for Kids (COPPA-Safe) is a practical book for adult creators who want original educational channels—not junk farms, not stolen cartoon worlds, and not creepy synthetic kids.

L. Merrick explains, in plain language:

- How COPPA thinking applies to your videos, site, and mailing list (parents, not children) - How to set YouTube made-for-kids flags honestly and why faking "not for kids" is a dead end - YouTube Kids and advertiser-friendly expectations that actually affect a classroom-style channel - Why you must invent your own characters, license music the hard way, and refuse famous IP - Where AI helps (outlines, drafts, calendars) and where it must stop (child voices, photorealistic children, bulk sludge, medical advice) - A production workflow, 90-day launch plan, phonics episode blueprint, and upload checklists - Monetization reality: limited kids CPMs, parent-sold printables, clean sponsorships, and no data selling

This book does not teach you to exploit children, collect kids' personal information, or create sexual or harmful content. It is not legal advice. Policies change; official FTC and YouTube pages win.

If you are willing to behave like a small curriculum publisher, this is the map. If you want a slot machine aimed at toddlers, this is not your book.
