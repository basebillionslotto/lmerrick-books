Make Money with AI: Songs on Spotify

Series: Make Money with AI

Ebook · $3.99 USD · L. Merrick

You can publish original music to Spotify with AI as a studio assistant. You cannot print money with bots.

Make Money with AI: Songs on Spotify is a practical book for adults who want to write, generate, finish, and distribute songs the way a small independent label would—not a spam farm.

L. Merrick explains, in plain language:

- How distributors (DistroKid, TuneCore, CD Baby, and peers) actually get tracks onto Spotify, Apple Music, and other stores - Where AI music tools help (sketches, stems, drafts) and where they fail (clones of famous artists, unvetted training claims, bulk sludge) - Copyright and human authorship: what you may own, what you may not, and why "the robot wrote it" is not a rights strategy - Platform rules against artificial streams, farms, and misleading metadata - Streaming math: pennies per thousand plays, not dollars per play—and why playlist luck is not a business plan - A 90-day release workflow, metadata checklists, and a catalog that looks like an artist, not a slot machine

This book does not teach stream farming, bot plays, fake playlists, or identity fraud. It is not legal, tax, or investment advice. Policies and rates change; official store, distributor, and Copyright Office pages win.

If you are willing to finish songs people might replay, this is the map. If you want a robot to mint a thousand tracks overnight, this is not your book.
