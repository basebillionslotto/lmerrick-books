Pediatrics: English–Spanish Dictionary for Physicians

Parent Interview, Anatomy, Pathology, Examination, Diagnosis, and Dosing Language

Series: English–Spanish Specialty Dictionaries for Physicians

Ebook · $9.99 USD · L. Merrick

A compact English–Spanish working lexicon for the pediatric clinic, urgent care, and inpatient ward. Chapters follow the visit: talking with a parent or caregiver, surface anatomy of the child, common pathology from newborn through adolescence, the physical exam (including developmental screening language), diagnosis and counseling phrases, and medication and dosing language labeled by INN/USAN.

Headwords are drawn from public MeSH, NIH MedlinePlus (including MedlinePlus en español), CDC immunization and case-definition wording, AAP public patient-education titles, Bright Futures open materials, WHO INN lists, and FDA pediatric labeling language—not from Nelson Textbook of Pediatrics or any commercial textbook glossary. Spanish includes Mexico, Andean, Caribbean, Southern Cone, and Spain variants where the everyday word changes, plus false-friend traps (*constipado*, *gripe*, *embarazada*).

Written for licensed clinicians who already know pediatrics and need usable Spanish at the bedside. It is a language aid, not a protocol and not a certified interpreter. Use a qualified interpreter for consent, safeguarding, and any conversation in which a misunderstanding could cause harm.
