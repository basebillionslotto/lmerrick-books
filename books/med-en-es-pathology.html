Pathology: English–Spanish Dictionary for Physicians

Specimens, Reports, Frozen Section, Autopsy, and INN Laboratory Adjuncts

Series: English–Spanish Specialty Dictionaries for Physicians

Ebook · $9.99 USD · L. Merrick

The report fails when the words fail. This digital lexicon gives physicians a public, specialty-organized English–Spanish vocabulary for pathology: the clinical history that must travel with the specimen, gross and anatomic language, general disease patterns, frozen-section and microscopic speech, stains and ancillary methods by public names, organ-system surgical pathology, cytopathology and hematopathology, autopsy consent language, and laboratory adjuncts listed by international nonproprietary name.

Entries are standard clinical terms—not a copy of any proprietary dictionary, CAP checklist, or vendor synoptic—with short notes on false friends (*gross*, *slide*, *margin*, *pending*, *positivo*), regional Spanish, and abbreviations that clash in bilingual charts (*PCR*, *ER*, *AP*). Drug and chemical tables name molecules only. There are no doses, stain recipes, or staging worksheets. It is a pocket for bilingual documentation and teaching. It is not medical advice, not a diagnosis, not a coding book, and not a certified translation. When consent, cancer disclosure, autopsy permission, or safety depends on exact wording, use a qualified interpreter and your institution’s forms.

Written as a digital-only professional reference in a matching series of English–Spanish specialty dictionaries for physicians.
