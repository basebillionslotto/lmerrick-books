Make Money with AI: Alibaba Dropshipping Store

A Professional Operator’s Guide to Niches, Suppliers, Storefronts, Advertising Math, and the Law

Series: Make Money with AI

Ebook · $3.99 USD · L. Merrick

Dropshipping from Alibaba is a thin-margin import-and-retail job, not a weekend script. This short professional how-to walks through niche selection, supplier vetting and samples, store setup (Shopify and peers), and the use of AI as a drafting tool for listings, ads, and customer service—with a hard ban on fake reviews, invented claims, and customs fiction.

It covers fulfillment and tracking, returns and chargebacks, and the advertising arithmetic (contribution, refunds, break-even CPA) that tells you when to stop spending. A legal chapter maps product safety (including children’s products and electrical hazards), FTC advertising and endorsement rules, intellectual property, privacy/pixels, and tax/duty basics. It is not legal advice.

Income is not guaranteed. Most new retail businesses lose money. The book is written for readers who will buy samples, tell the truth about shipping time, and shut a SKU that is unsafe or unprofitable.
