Anesthesiology: English–Spanish Dictionary for Physicians

Interview, Consent, Anatomy, Pathology, Exam, Airway, Procedures, and INN Medications

Series: English–Spanish Specialty Dictionaries for Physicians

Ebook · $9.99 USD · L. Merrick

The preoperative clinic and the operating room fail when the words fail. This digital lexicon gives physicians a public, specialty-organized English–Spanish vocabulary for anesthesiology: the interview and informed consent, fasting and comorbidity history, airway and neuraxial anatomy, pathology that changes the plan, the examination and monitors, ASA physical-status language, airway management and perioperative procedures, regional and neuraxial blocks, recovery and complications, and medicines listed by international nonproprietary name.

Entries are standard clinical terms—not a copy of any proprietary dictionary—with short notes on false friends (*constipado*, *embarazada*, *MAC*, *emergencia*), regional Spanish, and abbreviations that clash in bilingual charts. Drug tables name molecules only. There are no doses, infusion rates, or MAC percentages. It is a pocket for bilingual documentation and teaching. It is not medical advice, not a protocol, not a coding book, and not a certified translation. When consent or safety depends on exact wording, use a qualified interpreter and your institution’s forms.

Written as a digital-only professional reference in a matching series with other specialty volumes.
