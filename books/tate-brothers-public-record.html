The Tate Brothers: A Public Record

Andrew and Tristan Tate

Ebook · $2.99 USD · L. Merrick

The Tate brothers are among the most argued-about men of the 2020s. Almost none of the argument is a verdict.

*The Tate Brothers: A Public Record* is a digital-only work of court-record nonfiction by L. Merrick. It follows Andrew Tate and Tristan Tate through the documents that actually exist: a Romanian organized-crime investigation that produced a June 2023 indictment and a December 2024 appellate reversal; a second DIICOT file opened in 2024; British charging decisions that grew from 21 counts to a publicly reported 59; a Westminster civil ruling on frozen accounts; and July 2026 arrests in Miami on a United Kingdom extradition request.

The book does not treat social-media boasts as convictions. It does not name complainants. It does not describe alleged sexual acts. It uses *alleged* where the law has not found facts. As of August 22, 2026, both brothers are in U.S. federal detention pending extradition hearings—not serving a sentence on the Romanian or British sexual-offence files, which remain unfinished.

Written for readers who want dates, charging language, and sources rather than a fandom war.
