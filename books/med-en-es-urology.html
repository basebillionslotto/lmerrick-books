Urology: English–Spanish Dictionary for Physicians

A Working Lexicon for Clinic, Ward, and Operating Room

Series: English–Spanish Specialty Dictionaries for Physicians

Ebook · $9.99 USD · L. Merrick

Urology visits stall when the words stall. This digital lexicon gives physicians a public, specialty-organized English–Spanish vocabulary for the urinary tract and male genital system: interview language, anatomy, common pathology, the examination, office and operating-room procedures, drugs by international nonproprietary name, imaging and laboratory speech, counseling phrases, abbreviations, and a Spanish–English reverse index.

Entries are standard clinical terms—not a copy of any proprietary dictionary—with short notes on false friends, regional Spanish, and abbreviations that clash in bilingual charts. It is a pocket for bilingual documentation and teaching. It is not medical advice, not a dose table, not a coding book, and not a certified translation. When consent or safety depends on exact wording, use a qualified interpreter and your institution’s forms.

Written as a digital-only professional reference in a matching series of English–Spanish specialty dictionaries for physicians.
