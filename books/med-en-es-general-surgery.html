General Surgery: English–Spanish Dictionary for Physicians

Interview, Anatomy, Pathology, Exam, Perioperative Consent, Procedures, and Medications

Series: English–Spanish Specialty Dictionaries for Physicians

Ebook · $9.99 USD · L. Merrick

A compact English–Spanish working lexicon for the general-surgery clinic, ward, and operating room. Chapters follow the real order of a case: the interview (pain, last oral intake, prior operations, bowel habit, trauma), surface and visceral anatomy, presenting pathology, the physical exam, perioperative and consent stems, named procedures, and generic medications labeled by INN/USAN.

Headwords are drawn from public MeSH, NIH MedlinePlus (including MedlinePlus en español), WHO ICD and INN lists, ACS open patient-education titles, and CDC case-definition wording—not from Sabiston, Schwartz, Greenfield, Cameron, or any commercial textbook glossary. Spanish includes Mexico, Andean, Caribbean, Southern Cone, and Spain variants where the everyday word changes, plus false-friend traps (*constipado*, *vesícula* vs *vejiga*, *cura*, *ataque*).

Written for licensed clinicians who already know general surgery and need usable Spanish at the bedside. It is a language aid, not a protocol. Use a qualified interpreter for consent, stoma, mesh, cancer, and refusal conversations.
