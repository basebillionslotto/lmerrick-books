Vascular Surgery: English–Spanish Dictionary for Physicians

Interview, Anatomy, Pathology, Exam, Imaging, Perioperative Consent, Open and Endovascular Procedures, and Medications

Series: English–Spanish Specialty Dictionaries for Physicians

Ebook · $9.99 USD · L. Merrick

A compact English–Spanish working lexicon for the vascular clinic, ward, hybrid room, and operating theater. Chapters follow the real order of a case: the interview (claudication, rest pain, wounds, stroke warning signs, swelling, dialysis access), arterial and venous anatomy, presenting pathology, the physical exam, imaging and laboratory words, perioperative and consent stems, named open and endovascular procedures, generic medications labeled by INN/USAN, false-friend traps, and short clinic scripts.

Headwords are drawn from public MeSH, NIH MedlinePlus (including MedlinePlus en español), WHO ICD and INN lists, CDC case-definition wording, and open NIH patient-education titles on peripheral artery disease, aortic aneurysm, stroke signs, venous thrombosis, and hemodialysis access—not from Rutherford, Cronenwett, Sidawy, Moore, or any commercial textbook glossary. Spanish includes Mexico, Andean, Caribbean, Southern Cone, and Spain variants where the everyday word changes, plus chart traps (*derrame*, *ictus*, *constipado*, *CEA*, *vena* versus *arteria*).

Written for licensed clinicians who already know vascular surgery and need usable Spanish at the bedside. It is a language aid, not a protocol. Use a qualified interpreter for consent, amputation, aneurysm rupture risk, stroke risk, and refusal conversations.
