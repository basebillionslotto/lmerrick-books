Allergy and Immunology: English–Spanish Dictionary for Physicians

A Working Lexicon for Clinic, Skin Testing, and the Infusion Suite

Series: English–Spanish Specialty Dictionaries for Physicians

Ebook · $9.99 USD · L. Merrick

Allergy and immunology visits stall when the words stall. This digital lexicon gives physicians a public, specialty-organized English–Spanish vocabulary for hypersensitivity, asthma, food and drug allergy, immunodeficiency, and immune-modulating care: the allergy–immunology interview, immune and allergen language, disease families from rhinitis and eczema through anaphylaxis, mast-cell disease, and primary immunodeficiency, the examination, skin testing and challenge speech, laboratory and procedure phrases, drugs by international nonproprietary name, counseling and consent stems, abbreviations that clash in bilingual charts, and a Spanish–English reverse index.

Entries are standard clinical terms—not a copy of any proprietary dictionary—with short notes on false friends, regional Spanish, and words that patients hear as something else (*alergia* as any rash, *asma* as any cough, *prueba de sangre* as any IgE panel, *vacuna* as immunotherapy versus immunization, *shock* as any faint). It is a pocket for bilingual documentation and teaching. It is not medical advice, not a dose table, not a treatment protocol, not a coding book, and not a certified translation. When consent, challenge testing, epinephrine teaching, or immunodeficiency counseling depends on exact wording, use a qualified interpreter and your institution’s forms.

Written as a digital-only professional reference in a matching series of English–Spanish specialty dictionaries for physicians.
