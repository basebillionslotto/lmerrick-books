Critical Care: English–Spanish Dictionary for Physicians

Interview, Anatomy, Shock, Ventilation, Procedures, and INN Medications

Series: English–Spanish Specialty Dictionaries for Physicians

Ebook · $9.99 USD · L. Merrick

The intensive care unit fails when the words fail. This digital lexicon gives physicians a public, specialty-organized English–Spanish vocabulary for critical care: the interview with the patient and family, goals-of-care and interpreter language, airway and vascular anatomy, shock and organ-failure syndromes, the examination and monitors, mechanical ventilation and blood-gas speech, procedures and consent stems, and medicines listed by international nonproprietary name.

Entries are standard clinical terms—not a copy of any proprietary dictionary—with short notes on false friends (*shock*, *coma*, *intubado*, *sedado*, *código*), regional Spanish, and abbreviations that clash in bilingual charts. Drug tables name molecules only. There are no doses, infusion rates, or ventilator recipes. It is a pocket for bilingual documentation and teaching. It is not medical advice, not a protocol, not a coding book, and not a certified translation. When consent, limitation of life-sustaining treatment, or safety depends on exact wording, use a qualified interpreter and your institution’s forms.

Written as a digital-only professional reference in a matching series of English–Spanish specialty dictionaries for physicians.
