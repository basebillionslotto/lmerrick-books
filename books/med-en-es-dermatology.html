Dermatology: English–Spanish Dictionary for Physicians

Interview, Anatomy, Morphology, Pathology, Exam, Procedures, and Medications

Series: English–Spanish Specialty Dictionaries for Physicians

Ebook · $9.99 USD · L. Merrick

Clinic visits fail when the words for a rash fail. This digital lexicon gives physicians a public, specialty-organized English–Spanish vocabulary for the skin: the interview (itch, time course, exposures, drugs), surface anatomy and adnexa, primary and secondary lesions, inflammatory and infectious disease, pigment, hair and nails, bullous and autoimmune disorders, neoplasms, the examination and bedside tests, procedures from biopsy to Mohs and phototherapy, vehicles and drugs by international nonproprietary name, counseling phrases, abbreviations, and a Spanish–English reverse index.

Entries are standard clinical terms—not a copy of any proprietary dictionary—with short notes on false friends (*roncha*, *celulitis*, *culebrilla*, *ántrax*) and regional Spanish (Mexico, Spain, Andes, Southern Cone, Caribbean). It is a pocket for bilingual documentation and teaching. It is not medical advice, not a dose table, not a coding book, and not a certified translation. When consent, isotretinoin counseling, or safety depends on exact wording, use a qualified interpreter and your institution’s forms.

Written as a digital-only professional reference in a matching series of English–Spanish specialty dictionaries for physicians.
