Make Money with AI: Digital Products, Affiliates, and Influencers

Series: Make Money with AI

Ebook · $3.99 USD · L. Merrick

You can sell digital products, recommend tools, and publish social content without buying fake followers or hiding ads.

Make Money with AI: Digital Products, Affiliates, and Influencers is a practical book for adults who want a lawful, documented process: create something useful, disclose relationships, and treat language models as draft staff—not as a substitute for truth.

L. Merrick explains, in plain language:

- How to pick, price, deliver, and refund digital products without fake scarcity or stolen assets - How affiliate programs actually pay, what you must test before you recommend, and how to keep records - FTC endorsement rules in everyday words: material connections, clear disclosures, typical-results honesty, and platform placement - How to run a content calendar on social platforms without engagement pods, purchased comments, or undisclosed ads - Where AI helps (outlines, first drafts, checklists) and where it must stop (invented testimonials, medical claims, “I used this” when you did not) - A human-edit workflow, contract checklist for brand deals, 90-day operating plan, and a stop rule when the math or the ethics fail

This book does not teach fake followers, bot traffic, review fraud, or hidden advertising. It is not legal, tax, or income advice. Policies change; official FTC and platform pages win.

If you are willing to publish under your name and stand behind every claim, this is the map. If you want a ghost army of accounts, this is not your book.
