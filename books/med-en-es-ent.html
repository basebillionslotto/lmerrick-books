Otolaryngology: English–Spanish Dictionary for Physicians

Interview, Anatomy, Pathology, Exam, Procedures, and Medications

Series: English–Spanish Specialty Dictionaries for Physicians

Ebook · $9.99 USD · L. Merrick

Clinic visits, ward consults, and operating-room briefings fail when the words fail. This digital lexicon gives physicians a public, specialty-organized English–Spanish vocabulary for the ear, nose, sinus, pharynx, larynx, and neck: the interview, regional anatomy, common pathology, the office and bedside examination, office and operative procedures, drugs by international nonproprietary name, abbreviations, and a Spanish–English reverse index.

Entries are standard clinical terms—not a copy of any proprietary dictionary—with short notes on false friends, regional Spanish, and abbreviations that clash in bilingual charts (TM, ET, FESS, *constipado*, *gárgaras*). It is a pocket for bilingual documentation and teaching. It is not medical advice, not a dose table, not a coding book, and not a certified translation. When consent or safety depends on exact wording, use a qualified interpreter and your institution’s forms.

Written as a digital-only professional reference in a matching series of English–Spanish specialty dictionaries for physicians.
