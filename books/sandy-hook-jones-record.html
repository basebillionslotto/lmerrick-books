The Sandy Hook Defamation Record: Alex Jones in Court

Ebook · $2.99 USD · L. Merrick

A court-record account of the civil defamation cases that Sandy Hook families and a first responder brought against Alex Jones and Infowars-related companies—not a narrative of the 2012 murders, and not a forum for conspiracy claims.

Drawing on judgments, appellate opinions, bankruptcy dockets, and reporting by the Associated Press, Reuters, the New York Times, the Washington Post, and other major outlets, L. Merrick follows the cases from the 2018 filings through default judgments for discovery abuse, the 2022 Texas and Connecticut damages trials, Jones’s on-the-stand admission that the shooting was “100 percent real,” the Connecticut Appellate Court’s 2024 decision (including reversal of a CUTPA component), the U.S. Supreme Court’s October 2025 denial of review, Jones’s personal Chapter 7 liquidation, the stalled Infowars/Onion sale and receivership, and the Texas Third Court of Appeals’ August 21, 2026, reduction of the Heslin/Lewis punitive award to the statutory cap.

The book centers documented statements juries found defamatory, the First Amendment arguments Jones raised, and the unfinished problem of collection. As of this edition, major news organizations report that the families have not received distributions on the judgments. No photographs. Sources listed with URLs. Not a legal treatise and not authorized by any party.
