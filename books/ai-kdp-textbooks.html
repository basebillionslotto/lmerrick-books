Make Money with AI: Textbooks and KDP

Series: Make Money with AI

Ebook · $3.99 USD · L. Merrick

People can use AI to draft faster. They cannot use it as a license to clone textbooks, invent citations, or dump unvetted chapters into Kindle Direct Publishing.

*Make Money with AI: Textbooks and KDP* is a short, practical guide for adults who want to publish original instructional books—primers, workbooks, and course-style trade books—without plagiarism, metadata bait, or low-value public-domain tricks.

You will learn how to:

- Choose a subject you can defend intellectually, legally, and commercially - Research from sources you actually open, and take notes in your own words - Structure a finishable textbook instead of an encyclopedia or a blog collage - Cite, attribute, and respect copyright, public domain, and Creative Commons (including why NC licenses do not belong on a book you sell) - Use AI as an assistant for clarity and quizzes—not as an invisible author or a spinning machine - Meet KDP quality expectations readers and reviewers actually enforce - Pick honest categories and leftover keywords instead of hijacking browse nodes - Price a focused first edition at $3.99 with eyes open about royalty math - Run a 30-day path from positioning sentence to live listing, plus a pre-publish checklist

This book is not a scraped textbook, not exam-item dump advice, and not a plan to upload a catalog of interchangeable AI titles. It is process, checklists, and original teaching examples for one clean book you are willing to put a name on.

If you want a button that prints money, this is the wrong listing. If you want a method that survives a quality review, start here.
