Neurology: English–Spanish Dictionary for Physicians

A Working Lexicon for Wards, Clinics, and the Neurocritical-Care Unit

Series: English–Spanish Specialty Dictionaries for Physicians

Ebook · $9.99 USD · L. Merrick

Neurology consults fail when the words fail. This digital lexicon gives physicians a public, specialty-organized English–Spanish vocabulary for the nervous system: the interview (headache, seizure, weakness, stroke clock), neuroanatomy, pathology and disease families, the examination and public scale names, imaging and procedures, medications by international nonproprietary name, counseling stems, abbreviations that collide in bilingual charts, and dual indexes.

Entries are standard clinical terms—not a copy of any proprietary dictionary—with short notes on false friends, regional Spanish, and traps such as *mareo* versus *vértigo*, *ictus* versus *derrame*, and *ECG* meaning a heart tracing rather than a Glasgow score. It is a pocket for bilingual documentation and teaching. It is not medical advice, not a dose table, not a coding book, and not a certified translation. When consent, capacity, thrombolysis, or brain-death language depends on exact wording, use a qualified interpreter and your institution’s forms.

Written as a digital-only professional reference in a matching series of English–Spanish specialty dictionaries for physicians.
