AI Ethics in Medicine: Scribes, Practice, Privacy, and the Law

Series: AI in Medicine

Ebook · $9.99 USD · L. Merrick

Clinical AI is already in the exam room—ambient scribes, decision support, imaging models, and operational algorithms. This concise professional book explains how those tools differ from “AI as physician,” and what clinics must do about HIPAA, business associates, secondary use of PHI, bias and equity, liability, consent, and hallucinated notes. It synthesizes AMA augmented-intelligence principles, FDA SaMD/CDS and change-control guidance, WHO ethics of AI for health, HHS OCR HIPAA and Section 1557 materials, ONC algorithm-transparency rules, and major journal commentary from 2023–2026. Written for clinicians, administrators, compliance officers, and students. Not legal advice. Not medical advice.
