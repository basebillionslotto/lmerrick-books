Internal Medicine: English–Spanish Dictionary for Physicians

A bedside glossary of public-domain clinical terms for internists and trainees

Series: English–Spanish Specialty Dictionaries for Physicians

Ebook · $9.99 USD · L. Merrick

Internal medicine is a talking specialty. This concise English–Spanish dictionary gives internists and trainees the public-domain words they actually say on rounds, in clinic, and at discharge—without pretending to replace a certified interpreter.

L. Merrick organizes more than twenty short chapters the way a service is run: the interview and review of systems; hospital orders and the chart; cardiology, pulmonology, GI, hepatology, nephrology, endocrinology, hematology, oncology, infectious diseases, rheumatology, and neurology for the internist; geriatrics and palliative speech; procedures and consent stems; labs and imaging; allergy and skin; men’s and women’s health in the medical clinic; alcohol and other substances; nutrition and function; false friends; and pocket lists for rounds.

Each entry is an English term, a Spanish equivalent, and a brief note when gender, region, or a false friend matters (constipado is not constipation; acetaminophen is also paracetamol; PCR may mean C-reactive protein). Spoken lines are written in usted. Drug names are international nonproprietary names only. No doses, no proprietary lexicon, no copyrighted guideline text.

For attending physicians, residents, hospitalists, and clinic internists who already know the medicine and need the language. Use an interpreter for consent, capacity, and goodbye.
