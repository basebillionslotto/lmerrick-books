Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation: English–Spanish Dictionary for Physicians

A Working Lexicon for Inpatient Rehab, Clinic, EMG, and the Therapy Gym

Series: English–Spanish Specialty Dictionaries for Physicians

Ebook · $9.99 USD · L. Merrick

Rehab rounds fail when the words fail. This digital lexicon gives physicians a public, specialty-organized English–Spanish vocabulary for physical medicine and rehabilitation: the functional interview, anatomy of movement and disability, stroke and brain injury, spinal cord injury, amputation and prosthetics, musculoskeletal and pain medicine, the examination and public scale names, procedures including EMG and injections, medications by international nonproprietary name, counseling stems, abbreviations that collide in bilingual charts, and dual indexes.

Entries are standard clinical terms—not a copy of any proprietary dictionary—with short notes on false friends, regional Spanish, and traps such as *rehabilitación* versus street “rehab,” *discapacidad* versus outdated slurs, and *FIM* versus local functional scores. It is a pocket for bilingual documentation and teaching. It is not medical advice, not a dose table, not a coding book, and not a certified translation. When consent, capacity, or discharge language depends on exact wording, use a qualified interpreter and your institution’s forms.

Written as a digital-only professional reference in a matching series of English–Spanish specialty dictionaries for physicians.
