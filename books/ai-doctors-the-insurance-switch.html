AI Doctors: When Insurers Flip the Switch

Telehealth, Coverage, Exam Scores, and the Empathy Problem

Series: AI in Medicine

Ebook · $9.99 USD · L. Merrick

Telehealth did not become ordinary American care because cameras improved. It became ordinary when CMS and commercial payers paid for it—first under COVID-era section 1135 waivers, then under post-PHE extensions. This book uses that switch as a map for so-called AI doctors. It separates ambient scribes, clinical decision support, and autonomous diagnosis; reports published GPT-4, ChatGPT, and Med-PaLM 2 exam and MedQA results from the original papers; follows FDA-cleared imaging and pathology tools and the Esteva 2017 / Daneshjou 2022 dermatology arc; and treats the 2023 JAMA Internal Medicine chatbot “empathy” study as linguistic scores, not feeling. Six Sigma’s 3.4 DPMO is a manufacturing target, not a clinical forecast. Written for clinicians, administrators, and policy readers. Digital only. Not medical advice. Not legal advice.
