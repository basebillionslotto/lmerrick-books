Rheumatology: English–Spanish Dictionary for Physicians

A Working Lexicon for Clinic, Infusion Suite, and the Consult Service

Series: English–Spanish Specialty Dictionaries for Physicians

Ebook · $9.99 USD · L. Merrick

Rheumatology visits stall when the words stall. This digital lexicon gives physicians a public, specialty-organized English–Spanish vocabulary for joints, autoimmunity, and connective-tissue disease: the rheumatology interview, anatomy and immunology language, disease families from rheumatoid arthritis and spondyloarthritis through lupus, vasculitis, crystal disease, and myositis, the musculoskeletal examination, laboratory and imaging speech, procedures and infusion-suite phrases, drugs by international nonproprietary name, counseling and consent stems, abbreviations that clash in bilingual charts, and a Spanish–English reverse index.

Entries are standard clinical terms—not a copy of any proprietary dictionary—with short notes on false friends, regional Spanish, and words that patients hear as something else (*artritis* versus *artrosis*, *lupus* as a rash word, *biológico* as any injection, *constipado* versus constipation). It is a pocket for bilingual documentation and teaching. It is not medical advice, not a dose table, not a treatment protocol, not a coding book, and not a certified translation. When consent, infusion safety, or pregnancy counseling depends on exact wording, use a qualified interpreter and your institution’s forms.

Written as a digital-only professional reference in a matching series of English–Spanish specialty dictionaries for physicians.
