Geriatrics: English–Spanish Dictionary for Physicians

Comprehensive Geriatric Assessment, Function, Cognition, Falls, and Goals-of-Care Interview Language

Series: English–Spanish Specialty Dictionaries for Physicians

Ebook · $9.99 USD · L. Merrick

Geriatrics is a talking specialty. This concise English–Spanish dictionary gives geriatricians, hospitalists, and trainees the public-domain words they actually say in clinic, on the ward, in the skilled-nursing facility, and at the kitchen table—without pretending to replace a certified interpreter.

L. Merrick organizes short series chapters the way a geriatrics day is run: how to use the book; opening the visit and the comprehensive geriatric assessment; function, ADLs, and frailty; cognition, delirium, and dementia; falls, gait, and bones; polypharmacy and deprescribing language; heart and blood pressure in late life; lungs, sleep, and swallowing; nutrition and bowels; continence and kidneys; vision and hearing; mood, loneliness, and substances; pain and skin; goals of care, capacity, and advance directives; caregivers and elder safety; hospital and care transitions; infection and vaccines; endocrine language; laboratory and imaging phrases; medications by international nonproprietary name only; procedure consent stems; false friends; pocket lists; putting a visit together; and a Spanish–English reverse index.

Each entry is an English term, a Spanish equivalent, and a brief note when gender, region, or a false friend matters (*constipado* is not constipation; *delirio* is not automatically dementia; *anciano* is not always welcome). Spoken lines are written in *usted*. Drug names are international nonproprietary names only. No doses, no proprietary lexicon, no copyrighted guideline or screening-instrument text.

For attending geriatricians, residents, and teams who already know the medicine and need the language. Use an interpreter for consent, capacity, safeguarding, and goodbye.
