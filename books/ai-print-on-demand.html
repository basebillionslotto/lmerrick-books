Make Money with AI: Print-on-Demand Merch

Series: Make Money with AI

Ebook · $3.99 USD · L. Merrick

Print-on-demand looks like free money until you meet rejected uploads, thin margins, and ads that buy clicks instead of customers.

*Make Money with AI: Print-on-Demand Merch* is a short, practical field guide for building a small merch catalog with AI tools—without cloning brands, characters, or living artists. L. Merrick walks through how Printify, Printful, and Amazon Merch on Demand actually differ; how to write prompts and finish print files that survive review; how trademarks and publicity rules show up in real takedowns; and how to price a shirt so you know your break-even ROAS before you raise a daily budget.

This book does not include fake reviews, invented student results, or a promise that you will quit your job. It includes checklists, an ads scratchpad, and a 14-day evening build you can run against live costs from your own dashboard.

You will learn:

- When to choose a print network, a single fulfillment operator, or Amazon’s catalog - How contribution margin works after blanks, fees, and clicks - How to research gift-shaped niches that do not depend on a logo - How to keep type, contrast, and mockups honest - Why review gating, purchased ratings, and “gray area” knockoffs are account-suicide

For readers who want a clean process more than a hype reel.
