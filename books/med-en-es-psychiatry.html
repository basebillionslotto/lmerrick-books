Psychiatry: English–Spanish Dictionary for Physicians

Interview, mental status, pathology, exam, procedures, and medications (INN)

Series: English–Spanish Specialty Dictionaries for Physicians

Ebook · $9.99 USD · L. Merrick

Psychiatry is a talking specialty. This concise English–Spanish dictionary gives psychiatrists and trainees the public-domain words they actually say in clinic, on the ward, and in consults—without pretending to replace a certified interpreter.

L. Merrick organizes twenty-one short chapters the way a service is run: the interview; the mental status examination; mood, anxiety, trauma, obsessive-compulsive language, psychosis, personality and related terms; substance use; delirium and neurocognitive disorders; child and adolescent clinical vocabulary; geriatric psychiatry; safety and crisis language; the physical exam and named bedside tests (names only); procedures, neuromodulation, and consent stems; psychotropic medicines as international nonproprietary names only; capacity and systems-of-care words; counseling phrases; false friends; and dual pocket indexes.

Each entry is an English term, a Spanish equivalent, and a brief note when gender, region, or a false friend matters (*delirio* is not always delirium; *constipado* is not constipation; *loco* is not a diagnosis). Spoken lines are written in usted. Drug names are INNs only. No doses, no copyrighted scale items, no proprietary lexicon, no diagnostic-criteria text.

Clinical, person-first, respectful language. For attending physicians, residents, consult liaisons, and clinic psychiatrists who already know the medicine and need the language. Use an interpreter for consent, capacity, involuntary care, and goodbye.
