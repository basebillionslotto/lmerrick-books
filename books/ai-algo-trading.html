Make Money with AI: Stocks and Algorithmic Trading

A Plain-Language Guide to Data, Backtests, Brokers, Paper Trading, Risk, and the Rules

Series: Make Money with AI

Ebook · $3.99 USD · L. Merrick

Algorithmic trading is not a secret formula and not a promise. It is a stack of data, rules, tests, brokers, and law. This short educational book explains that stack in ordinary language for readers who want to understand how automated stock strategies are designed and evaluated—and why most of them fail.

Chapters cover what an algorithm actually does with an order; where market data comes from and how it lies; how a backtest is built and how it is gamed; overfitting and look-ahead bias; choosing a broker and using paper trading; position sizing, drawdowns, and operational risk; and the U.S. regulatory frame that applies to people who trade, advertise, or manage other people’s money. It states, repeatedly, that nothing here is investment advice, that no system is guaranteed to beat the market, and that this book does not help anyone evade securities law.

Written as a digital-only nonfiction brief. Educational only.
