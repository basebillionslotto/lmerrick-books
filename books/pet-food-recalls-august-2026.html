Two Recalls: Fromm, Oma’s Pride, and the August 2026 Pet-Food Alerts

A Public-Record Briefing on Two FDA Company Announcements

Ebook · $2.99 USD · L. Merrick

On August 12, 2026, Oma’s Pride (Miller Foods, Inc., Avon, Connecticut) voluntarily recalled one lot of frozen raw Woof Complete Canine Chicken Recipe — 6 lb bag, lot BB012729 — after an FDA-collected sample tested positive for Salmonella. The company counted 639 bags, named eleven states plus frozen direct-to-consumer orders, and the FDA-linked notice, as USA Today reported on August 19, was updated on August 17 to say three illnesses were in dogs and that no human illnesses had been reported. On August 21, Fromm Family Foods LLC of Mequon, Wisconsin, separately recalled 3,852 cases of Fromm Turkey Pâté and 1,973 cases of Diner Classics Milo’s Meatloaf Pâté for potential metal contamination (lots EP2A3306 551006 and EP2A3306 551029, Best By 032029), stating no reports of illness or injury. This digital briefing transcribes those company announcements as posted by FDA and republished by Florida DACS and EIN Presswire, keeps the two hazards in separate files, and does not invent illness counts, can totals, or root causes. Not medical or veterinary advice. Lot codes as published. Digital only.
