Family Medicine: English–Spanish Dictionary for Physicians

Continuity Clinic, Prevention, Chronic Disease, and Whole-Family Interview Language

Series: English–Spanish Specialty Dictionaries for Physicians

Ebook · $9.99 USD · L. Merrick

Family medicine is a talking specialty across the life course. This concise English–Spanish dictionary gives family physicians and trainees the public-domain words they actually say in continuity clinic, on the phone, at well visits, and at hospital follow-up—without pretending to replace a certified interpreter.

L. Merrick organizes short series chapters the way a family-medicine day is run: how to use the book; the continuity interview and review of systems; prevention, vaccines, and screening language; cardiometabolic disease; lungs and ENT in the clinic; gastroenterology; endocrinology including diabetes teaching; women’s health and prenatal care in family practice; men’s health; pediatrics for the family physician; musculoskeletal and sports complaints; dermatology; mental health and substance use; geriatrics and home context; procedures and consent stems; laboratory and imaging phrases; medications by international nonproprietary name only; social context and access; false friends; pocket lists; and a Spanish–English reverse index.

Each entry is an English term, a Spanish equivalent, and a brief note when gender, region, or a false friend matters (*constipado* is not constipation; *embarazada* is pregnant, not embarrassed). Spoken lines are written in *usted*. Drug names are international nonproprietary names only. No doses, no proprietary lexicon, no copyrighted guideline text.

For attending family physicians, residents, and clinic teams who already know the medicine and need the language. Use an interpreter for consent, capacity, safeguarding, and goodbye.
