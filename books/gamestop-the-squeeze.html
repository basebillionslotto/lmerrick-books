The Squeeze: GameStop, Roaring Kitty, and the Short That Went Public

A Documented Account of January 2021, the Hearing, and What Followed

Ebook · $2.99 USD · L. Merrick

In the last week of January 2021, a New York Stock Exchange ticker that had spent years as a specialty-retail story became a public event. GameStop Corp., trading as GME, was already familiar to anyone who had walked a mall: a chain that sold new and used video-game hardware, software, and accessories, plus an expanding catalog of digital codes and collectibles. What was new was the scale of attention. Individual investors discussed the name on social media; television covered the price; members of Congress scheduled hearings; and the staff of the Securities and Exchange Commission later wrote a report devoted largely to a single month of trading in a single stock.
