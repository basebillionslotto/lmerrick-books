Radiology: English–Spanish Dictionary for Physicians

A Working Lexicon for Reading Rooms, Consent, and Interventional Suites

Series: English–Spanish Specialty Dictionaries for Physicians

Ebook · $9.99 USD · L. Merrick

Imaging interviews, consent talks, and report read-backs fail when the words fail. This digital lexicon gives physicians a public, specialty-organized English–Spanish vocabulary for diagnostic and interventional radiology: the imaging interview and consent, regional anatomy as seen on studies, common pathology descriptors, examination and positioning language, procedures, contrast media and related medications by international nonproprietary name, and bilingual indexes.

Entries are standard clinical terms—not a copy of any proprietary dictionary or reporting atlas—with short notes on false friends, regional Spanish, and abbreviations that clash in bilingual charts (CT, MR, *contrast* versus *contraste*, *film* versus *película*). It is a pocket for bilingual documentation and teaching. It is not medical advice, not a dose table, not a coding book, not a structured-reporting manual, and not a certified translation. When consent or safety depends on exact wording, use a qualified interpreter and your institution’s forms.

Written as a digital-only professional reference in a matching series with other specialty volumes.
