Make Money with AI: Local Service Agency

Series: Make Money with AI

Ebook · $3.99 USD · L. Merrick

Local shops do not need another lecture about “the future of AI.” They need a site that works on a phone, a way to book after 6 p.m., and ads that stop when the math fails.

*Make Money with AI: Local Service Agency* is a short field guide for a one-person or two-person firm that sells a defined stack—website, chat or SMS intake, booking, and listings/ads hygiene—to owner-operated businesses. L. Merrick covers offers, pricing bands, statements of work, account ownership, ad arithmetic, and the lines you do not cross (fake reviews, invented case studies, medical or legal advice in a bot).

This book does not include testimonials, star ratings, or “results from my students.” It includes discovery questions, launch and retainer checklists, sample clause topics for counsel to review, and a 30-day evening start plan.

You will learn:

- How to package Presence, Intake, and Demand without selling a magic model - How to set setup fees and retainers so you are not unpaid IT - How to keep domains, ads accounts, and Google profiles in the *client’s* name - How to use AI for drafts and still fact-check hours, licenses, and claims - Why lead guarantees, fake reviews, and hostage logins end firms

For readers who want contracts and unit economics more than a hype reel.
