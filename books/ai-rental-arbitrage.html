Make Money with AI: Rental Arbitrage

Series: Make Money with AI

Ebook · $3.99 USD · L. Merrick

Rental arbitrage is a hospitality spread: you control a home under a lease that *allows* lodging, then sell nights for more than your all-in cost. Artificial intelligence can help you price, message guests, and keep books. It cannot make a forbidden use legal.

*Make Money with AI: Rental Arbitrage* by L. Merrick is a practical, conservative guide to the model as a real small business—not a loophole. You will learn how the economics actually work, how to structure written landlord permission, how short-term rental platforms fit (and what their rules are for), how to use AI for pricing and operations without automating illegal decisions, and how insurance, local licensing, and taxes decide whether a deal is even real.

This book does not teach lease fraud, hidden sublets, fake accounts, or terms-of-service evasion. If a lease, HOA, city, or platform forbids the use, the strategy is to get permission, change property, or walk away.

Includes a 90-day legal launch sequence, landlord-packet outline, insurance questions for a licensed broker, tax topics to take to a CPA, and hypothetical numbers that show why many “easy money” spreadsheets fail.

Educational only. Not legal, tax, or investment advice. No income promises.
