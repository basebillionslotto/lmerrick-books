You do not need a warehouse, an app, or a bought list. You need a specific reader, a weekly artifact, and permission.

*Make Money with AI: Newsletters and Paid Communities* is a field manual for building a real publication on Substack or Beehiiv, then opening a paid member room people actually use. It shows how to use AI to draft, outline, and edit—without publishing slop, fake case studies, or invented testimonials.

You will learn how to:

- Pick a niche with a budget, a named reader, and a content engine - Choose Substack or Beehiiv for the job you are actually doing - Run an AI draft-and-edit loop where you remain the author - Grow the first subscribers through outreach, referrals, and guest work—not purchased emails - Stay on the right side of CAN-SPAM, CASL, and GDPR-style permission rules (this is not legal advice) - Design a paid community with onboarding, cadence, and rules so it does not become a ghost town - Sequence money: memberships, honest sponsorships, affiliates you have used, and services that do not kill the calendar - Follow a ninety-day plan from first issue to a founding paid offer

This book is blunt about what does not work: fake subscriber buys, scraped lists, engagement farms, and “AI growth agents” that want your CSV. Trust is the asset. Models are staff.

If you can write a clear paragraph and talk to fifty humans, you can run this stack. Read it once. Then send issue one.
