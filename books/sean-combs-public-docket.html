The Public Docket: Sean Combs

Charges, Detention, Split Verdict, Sentence, and Civil Filings, 2023–2026

Ebook · $2.99 USD · L. Merrick

A court-record account of *United States v. Sean Combs* and the public civil docket that surrounded it.

In July 2025 a Manhattan federal jury acquitted Sean Combs of racketeering conspiracy and of two counts of sex trafficking, and convicted him of two counts of transportation to engage in prostitution. In October 2025 he was sentenced to 50 months, a $500,000 fine, and five years of supervised release. As of August 22, 2026, his appeal to the Second Circuit remained undecided.

This book states charges as filed, detention and trial outcomes as courts announced them, verdicts as returned, civil suits as filed, and settlements only when public. Accusations that were not adjudicated are labeled as allegations. It is not a true-crime recreation and does not contain graphic sexual narrative.
