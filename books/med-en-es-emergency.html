Emergency Medicine: English–Spanish Dictionary for Physicians

Interview, Anatomy, Pathology, Exam, Resuscitation, Procedures, and Medications

Series: English–Spanish Specialty Dictionaries for Physicians

Ebook · $9.99 USD · L. Merrick

A compact English–Spanish working lexicon for the emergency department. Chapters follow the real order of a shift: the interview (OPQRST, SAMPLE, safety), surface anatomy, presenting pathology, the physical exam, triage and resuscitation language, procedures and consent phrases, and generic medications labeled by INN/USAN.

Headwords are drawn from public MeSH, NIH MedlinePlus (including MedlinePlus en español), CDC case-definition wording, ACEP open educational titles, AHA public campaign language, and WHO INN lists—not from Tintinalli, Rosen, or any commercial textbook glossary. Spanish includes Mexico, Andean, Caribbean, Southern Cone, and Spain variants where the everyday word changes, plus false-friend traps (*constipado*, *ataque*, *aborto*).

Written for licensed clinicians who already know emergency medicine and need usable Spanish at the bedside. It is a language aid, not a protocol. Use a qualified interpreter for consent, AMA, and end-of-life conversations.
